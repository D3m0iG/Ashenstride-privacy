## English

**Last updated:** 21 November 2026  
**Developer / controller:** Luis Barros (individual developer, Portugal) — contact: **luis.baf0z@gmail.com**

**Summary.** Ashenstride has no ads and no usage analytics, and you can play all of it without any account. Your progress and your steps stay on your phone, unless you choose one of the optional online features described below. The game sends **anonymous crash and freeze reports** (Firebase Crashlytics, Google) so we can fix problems quickly; this is **on by default and you can switch it off at any time** in Settings → Data. Only if you turn on the optional "Cloud save" feature (by signing in with Google Play Games; off by default), the game uploads a copy of your game save to **your own Google account** through Google Play Games, so you can restore it on another phone. Only if you choose to use the optional **Friends** feature, the game creates a player account for you and keeps a small profile on our server (nickname, level, avatar, banner, friend code and your friends list; see section 3c); you can delete it at any time. Inside Friends there are two more choices, each one separate: the **step ranking** and the **clans**. Only if you join them, the game sends the **number of steps** it counted for you, to be seen by your friends (ranking) or by the members of your clan. Your hero (a portrait, your look, the equipment you wear, your attributes and the name of your clan) is shown to your friends unless you hide it and, only if you turn on **phone notices**, the game stores a device code on our server so that you can be alerted about friend requests and gifts even when the game is closed. If you buy an optional in-game item, Google Play handles the payment. We, the developer, do not receive, see or store the cloud copy or your payment details, and we do not sell any of your data.

### 1. What the app does on your phone
- **Steps.** Read from Android's activity sensor ("Physical activity" permission) and, when available, from Health Connect (permission to **read** steps only; the app never writes to Health Connect). Steps become in-game progress (travel, encounters, rewards). They are processed **only on your phone**. They are never sent anywhere, with one exception that only happens if you choose it: if you join the friends' step ranking or a clan (section 3c), the game sends step **counts** (how many steps per day), never the sensor or Health Connect records themselves.
- **Background step reading.** If you turn on phone alerts, the app periodically reads aggregated steps from Health Connect even when the game is closed, only to notify you when your hero arrives somewhere. You can turn it off in Settings → "Phone alerts".
- **Notifications** (with your permission; quiet hours 10 pm–8 am by default) and **vibration** (can be turned off in Settings). Notices about friend requests and gifts exist only if you turn on "Phone notices" in Friends (section 3c); the game's other alerts are created by the game on your phone, without any server.
- **Game data** (progress, inventory, settings) is stored in the app's private storage on your phone.

### 2. Backups of your progress
- **Android backup (Google).** Android may back up the game's saved data to **your own Google account** (Google Drive) so it can be restored on a new phone. This is done by Android and Google, under your Google account settings and Google's privacy policy; the developer does not receive or have access to it. You can turn it off in your phone's Google backup settings.
- **Export / import.** In Settings → Data you can export your progress to a file that **you** choose where to save, and import it on another phone. The file only goes where you put it.
- **Cloud save (optional, off by default).** If you tap **"Sign in with Google Play Games"** (on the first screen of the game or in Settings → Google Play account), the game signs you in to **Google Play Games** and uploads a copy of your game save for each character to your Google account (Play Games "Saved Games"). The copy contains your character's progress (level, inventory, map and story progress), your Wayfarer Pass progress and the rewards unlocked for all your characters (so that a new character can collect them), and the game's own step counters (how many steps the game has counted, and the technical data the game keeps to avoid counting the same steps twice, which can include the names of the apps that supplied your steps through Health Connect). It does not contain your location, contacts or the raw Health Connect records. The copy is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and is stored and processed by Google under your Google account settings and Google's privacy policy; the developer has no access to it. Only the campaign you play for real is uploaded. While you are signed in, the game shows your Play Games gamer name on the screen; the game does not store it or send it anywhere. After you sign in once, the game signs in again by itself every time you open it, until you tap **"Sign out"** (on the first screen or in Settings → Google Play account). You can sign out at any time (this stops new uploads; the existing copy stays until you delete it) and you can delete the cloud copy in Settings → Data → "Delete cloud copy", in the Google Play Games app, or in your Google Account. Google Play Games may also collect diagnostics and analytics for its own service, as described in Google's documentation. The game only starts this once you turn the feature on.

### 3. Internet, purchases and third parties
The app uses the Internet **only** for (a) anonymous crash and freeze reports (see section 3a), (b) the optional cloud save described above, and only after you turn it on, (c) optional purchases (diamonds and the paid tier of the Wayfarer Pass) through **Google Play Billing**, (d) downloading game news (section 3b), and (e) the optional Friends feature (section 3c), and only after you enter it, including, only if you turn them on, notices about friend requests and gifts through **Firebase Cloud Messaging** (section 3c). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play, under Google's terms and privacy policy; the developer does not receive your card or payment details, only the confirmation that a purchase was made. For the paid tier of the Wayfarer Pass, the game asks Google Play whether your Google account owns it, so that it can be given back to you on another phone or on a new character; the game keeps only "owns it / does not own it". The app does not contain advertising or usage analytics. The third parties involved are Google (Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Play Games Services and Google Play Billing) and GitHub, which hosts this website and the news file (section 3b). Nothing else is sent to the developer.

### 3a. Anonymous crash and freeze reports (Firebase Crashlytics)
**On by default.** To fix crashes and freezes quickly, the app sends anonymous crash and freeze reports to **Firebase Crashlytics (Google)** as soon as they happen. You can switch this off at any time in **Settings → Data → "Send anonymous crash reports"**; after that nothing more is sent. The game tells you about this the first time you open it.
- **What is sent:** the technical error (where in the code it failed), how long a freeze lasted and on which screen, the game version, your phone model, the Android version, the memory state at the time of the failure, and an installation identifier generated by Firebase (it does not identify you personally).
- **What the game never puts in a report:** your name, e-mail, Google Play Games ID, location, step counts, progress, inventory or save files.
- **Who receives it:** Google acts as our service provider (processor) for this. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy. Because a report is an ordinary Internet request, Google's servers see your IP address in the process; the game does not add it to the report.
- **Why (legal basis):** our legitimate interest in keeping the game stable, with an easy switch-off.

### 3b. Game news
The game shows news about itself (the **"News"** button on the map). Some news come with the game; the others are downloaded from a small public file on this website (`https://d3m0ig.github.io/Ashenstride-privacy/news.json`), hosted by **GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.)**. The game only **downloads** that file: it sends nothing about you, your phone or your game. As with any Internet request, GitHub's servers see your IP address in the process (see GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Which news you have already seen is stored only on your phone.

### 3c. Friends (optional)
The game works completely without this. Nothing described here happens until you open **Settings → Friends** and tap **"Enter friends"**.
- **Player account.** When you enter, the game signs you in to **Firebase Authentication (Google)** with your Google Play Games profile. This creates a player account with an identifier generated by Firebase. Google keeps with it your Play Games player ID and the public Play Games profile that Google supplies at sign-in (your gamer name and the address of your profile picture), the dates of creation and of your last sign-in and, for security, the IP address used. The game does not receive your e-mail address.
- **Protection against abuse.** While you use Friends, the game asks **Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity (Google)** to confirm that its requests come from the genuine game, installed from Google Play, on a genuine device. To do this, Google receives technical information about the app and the device. We do not receive that information: the game only gets a short-lived proof that the check passed, which it attaches to its requests.
- **Profile.** Only if you then choose to create a profile, we store in **Cloud Firestore (Google, servers in the European Union)**: the **nickname** you choose (up to 24 characters), your hero's **level**, an **avatar** (one of 12 colours), the **banner** you chose in the game, a random **friend code**, and the date of the last change.
- **Friends, requests, blocks and gifts.** We store who your friends are and since when, friend requests you sent or received that are still waiting, the players you blocked, and gifts waiting to be collected (who gave them and on which day).
- **Who can see what.** Your friends, people you sent a request to or who sent you one, and anyone you give your friend code to can see your nickname, level, avatar and banner. Nobody can browse or search the list of players: a profile can only be opened with its exact code. Your steps, location, e-mail and game progress are never part of the profile; step counts are shared only if you join the ranking or a clan (the next two points).
- **Reports.** If a player reports another one, we store who reported, who was reported, the reason chosen from a fixed list, the day and the nickname the reported player had at that moment. Only the developer can read reports, and uses them only to moderate (for example to remove an offensive nickname or to close an account). Reports are kept for up to 12 months.
- **Step ranking among friends (a separate choice).** Only if you tap **"Join the ranking"**, we store the number of steps the game counted for you on each of the last 7 days. Only you and your friends can see those numbers. **"Leave the ranking…"** deletes them from the server at once.
- **Clans and the weekly raid (a separate choice).** Only if you create or join a clan, we store your place in it: your nickname, level and banner, the day you joined, and the number of steps the game counted for you today and this week (they are the clan's damage to the weekly boss). The members of your clan (10 at most) can see each other's nickname, level, banner and those step counts. By default they also see your hero's **look** (hair, beard, equipped pieces); this is cosmetic only and you can switch it off in the clan screen ("Show my look to the clan"). The profile of the clan's leader (nickname, level, avatar, banner and friend code) can be opened by any player who can read the clan, because the clan record holds the leader's player identifier; the friend code only allows sending a friend request, and you can block anyone. We also store the clan itself (its name, the description written by its leader, how many members it has, when it was created and which player account leads it) and the week in which you last collected a raid reward. The name, description and size of a clan can be seen and searched by every player who entered Friends; the list of its members cannot. Leaving the clan deletes your place in it; when the last member leaves, the clan is deleted. A member can report the clan's name or description; the report is stored as described above, against the clan's leader, with the name of the clan.
- **Your hero, shown to your friends (on by default; you can hide it).** So that friends can open your profile, the game stores a small **portrait** of your hero in your profile (your hair and beard and the chest piece you wear; the helmet only if you choose to show it) and a **hero card** that only your friends can read: your hero's full **look** (hair, beard and the cosmetic pieces you wear), the **equipment you actually wear** (for each slot: the item, its rarity and its material), your five **attributes** (numbers) and your **clan** (only its identifier, to show its name). It is information only and gives nothing in the game. In **Settings → Friends** you can switch it off ("Show my hero to friends": the card and the portrait are deleted from the server and your profile goes back to a plain coloured avatar) and choose whether the portrait shows the helmet.
- **Phone notices (a separate choice, off by default).** Only if you turn on **"Phone notices"** in Friends and allow notifications in Android, the game asks **Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google)** for a **device token** (a code that identifies your phone for notices, not you) and stores it in **Cloud Firestore**, so that a server function can alert you when you receive a friend request or a gift, even when the game is closed. The message that travels through Google contains **only the type** (request or gift): no names, texts or friend codes. The text you see is written by the game on your phone, in your language, and by default no notice is shown between 10 pm and 8 am. To prevent abuse, the server also keeps for each player small counters (the day, how many notices were sent that day and when the last one of each type was sent). Turning notices off in Friends deletes the token on your phone and on our server; **"Delete my social data…"** or deleting your account also deletes the counters.
- **There is no chat.** Players cannot write messages to each other; the only free texts are the nickname and the name and description of a clan.
- **How long.** Until you delete it. **Settings → Friends → "Delete my social data…"** deletes your profile, friend code, friendships, requests, unclaimed gifts, blocks, your ranking steps, your hero card and portrait, your phone-notices device code and counters, your place in your clan, the record of your raid rewards and the player account itself. Reports are the exception described above.
- **Who processes it:** Google, as our service provider (processor). See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
- **Why (legal basis):** your consent, which you give by entering the feature and creating a profile, and which you withdraw by deleting your data.

### 4. E-mail
If you write to luis.baf0z@gmail.com, we receive your e-mail address and whatever you write, and we use them only to answer you.

### 5. Your choices and rights
- Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings → Apps → Ashenstride → Storage → "Clear data", removes everything stored on your phone by the app. A cloud copy, if you made one, stays in your Google account until you delete it (Settings → Data → "Delete cloud copy", the Google Play Games app, or your Google Account).
- You can revoke the step and notification permissions at any time in Android or Health Connect settings.
- Unless you use the optional Friends feature, the developer holds no personal data about you (crash reports are anonymous and held by Google as our service provider), so there is nothing for us to delete, correct or export; for the cloud copy use the options above. If you use Friends, you can see and change your profile in Settings → Friends, delete everything there, or ask us by e-mail for a copy or for deletion (tell us your friend code). If you believe we hold data about you (for example from an e-mail you sent), write to us and we will answer. You may also complain to your data-protection authority (in Portugal: CNPD, cnpd.pt).

### Delete your data (Ashenstride, by Luis Barros)
Full instructions, including how to ask for deletion without the app: **[Delete your data](apagar-dados)**.

**Friends data and player account (only if you used the optional Friends feature).** In the game: **Settings → Friends → "Delete my social data…"**. This deletes your profile, friend code, friendships, requests, unclaimed gifts, blocks, your ranking steps, your hero card and portrait, your phone-notices device code and counters, your place in your clan and your player account. If you no longer have the game, write to luis.baf0z@gmail.com with your friend code and we delete it within 30 days.

**Cloud copy.** Apart from that, the only data that can exist outside your phone is the optional **cloud copy** of your game save in **your own Google account**. To delete it:
1. In the game: **Settings → Data → "Delete cloud copy"**; or
2. In the **Google Play Games** app: your profile → Settings → "Delete Play Games account and data" (choose Ashenstride); or
3. In your **Google Account** (myaccount.google.com → Data & privacy → Third-party apps and services → Ashenstride).
To remove everything stored on your phone, uninstall the app or use Android Settings → Apps → Ashenstride → Storage → "Clear data". You can also ask by e-mail (luis.baf0z@gmail.com) and we will help you.

### 6. Permissions
Physical activity (step sensor), Health Connect steps (read only), notifications (the game's alerts and, only if you turn them on, notices about friend requests and gifts), vibration and **Internet (only for anonymous crash reports, the optional cloud save, the optional Friends feature, purchases through Google Play and downloading game news)**. The app has no location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permission.

### 7. Children
The app is not directed at children under 13. The optional Friends feature has no chat; the only texts other players can see are the nickname and the name and description of a clan, which can be reported.

### 8. Changes
If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.

### 3. Internet, compras e terceiros

A app não contém publicidade nem estatísticas de utilização. Os terceiros envolvidos são a Google (Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Play Games Services e Google Play Billing) e o GitHub, que aloja este site e o ficheiro das novidades (secção 3b). Mais nada é enviado ao programador.

### 3a. Relatórios anónimos de falhas e bloqueios (Firebase Crashlytics)
**Ligado por defeito.** Para corrigir falhas e bloqueios depressa, a app envia relatórios anónimos de falhas e bloqueios para o **Firebase Crashlytics (Google)** assim que acontecem. Podes desligar a qualquer momento em **Definições → Dados → «Enviar relatórios de falhas anónimos»**; depois disso não é enviado mais nada. O jogo avisa-te disto na primeira vez que o abres.
- **O que é enviado:** o erro técnico (em que parte do código falhou), quanto durou um bloqueio e em que ecrã, a versão do jogo, o modelo do telemóvel, a versão do Android, o estado da memória no momento da falha e um identificador de instalação gerado pelo Firebase (não te identifica pessoalmente).
- **O que o jogo nunca põe num relatório:** o teu nome, e-mail, ID do Google Play Games, localização, passos, progresso, inventário ou saves.
- **Quem recebe:** a Google, como prestador de serviços do programador. Ver https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy. Como um relatório é um pedido normal de Internet, os servidores da Google veem o teu endereço IP; o jogo não o junta ao relatório.
- **Porquê (base legal):** o nosso interesse legítimo em manter o jogo estável, com um interruptor fácil para desligar.

### 3b. Novidades do jogo
O jogo mostra novidades sobre si próprio (o botão **«Novidades»** no mapa). Algumas vêm com o jogo; as outras são descarregadas de um pequeno ficheiro público deste site (`https://d3m0ig.github.io/Ashenstride-privacy/news.json`), alojado no **GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.)**. O jogo só **descarrega** esse ficheiro: não envia nada sobre ti, o teu telemóvel ou o teu jogo. Como em qualquer pedido de Internet, os servidores do GitHub veem o teu endereço IP (ver a declaração de privacidade do GitHub: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Que novidades já viste fica guardado só no teu telemóvel.

### 3c. Amigos (opcional)
O jogo funciona todo sem isto. Nada do que aqui se descreve acontece até abrires **Definições → Amigos** e carregares em **«Entrar nos amigos»**.
- **Conta de jogador.** Ao entrar, o jogo inicia sessão no **Firebase Authentication (Google)** com o teu perfil do Google Play Games. Isto cria uma conta de jogador com um identificador gerado pelo Firebase. A Google guarda com ela o teu ID de jogador do Play Games e o perfil público do Play Games que a Google fornece no início de sessão (o teu nome de jogador e o endereço da tua imagem de perfil), as datas de criação e do último início de sessão e, por segurança, o endereço IP usado. O jogo não recebe o teu endereço de e-mail.
- **Proteção contra abusos.** Enquanto usas os Amigos, o jogo pede ao **Firebase App Check com o Google Play Integrity (Google)** que confirme que os pedidos vêm do jogo verdadeiro, instalado pelo Google Play, num dispositivo verdadeiro. Para isso, a Google recebe informação técnica sobre a app e o dispositivo. Nós não recebemos essa informação: o jogo recebe apenas uma prova de curta duração de que a verificação passou, que junta aos seus pedidos.
- **Perfil.** Só se depois escolheres criar um perfil, guardamos no **Cloud Firestore (Google, servidores na União Europeia)**: a **alcunha** que escolheres (até 24 caracteres), o **nível** do teu herói, um **avatar** (uma de 12 cores), o **banner** que escolheste no jogo, um **código de amigo** aleatório e a data da última alteração.
- **Amigos, pedidos, bloqueios e presentes.** Guardamos quem são os teus amigos e desde quando, os pedidos de amizade que enviaste ou recebeste e ainda esperam resposta, os jogadores que bloqueaste e os presentes por apanhar (quem os deu e em que dia).
- **Quem vê o quê.** Os teus amigos, as pessoas a quem enviaste um pedido ou que to enviaram, e quem tiver o teu código de amigo vêem a tua alcunha, o teu nível, o teu avatar e o teu banner. Ninguém consegue percorrer nem pesquisar a lista de jogadores: um perfil só se abre com o código exato. Os teus passos, a tua localização, o teu e-mail e o teu progresso no jogo nunca fazem parte do perfil; as contagens de passos só são partilhadas se entrares no ranking ou num clã (os dois pontos seguintes).
- **Denúncias.** Se um jogador denunciar outro, guardamos quem denunciou, quem foi denunciado, o motivo escolhido de uma lista fixa, o dia e a alcunha que o denunciado tinha nesse momento. Só o programador lê as denúncias, e usa-as apenas para moderar (por exemplo, retirar uma alcunha ofensiva ou fechar uma conta). As denúncias são guardadas até 12 meses.
- **Ranking de passos entre amigos (uma escolha à parte).** Só se carregares em **«Participar no ranking»**, guardamos o número de passos que o jogo contou em cada um dos últimos 7 dias. Só tu e os teus amigos vêem esses números. **«Sair do ranking…»** apaga-os logo do servidor.
- **Clãs e raide semanal (uma escolha à parte).** Só se criares um clã ou entrares num, guardamos o teu lugar nele: a tua alcunha, o teu nível e o teu banner, o dia em que entraste e o número de passos que o jogo contou hoje e nesta semana (são o dano do clã ao boss da semana). Os membros do teu clã (10 no máximo) vêem uns dos outros a alcunha, o nível, o banner e essas contagens de passos. Por defeito vêem também o **aspeto** do teu herói (cabelo, barba, peças equipadas); é só cosmético e podes desligá-lo no ecrã do clã («Mostrar o meu aspeto ao clã»). O perfil do líder do clã (alcunha, nível, avatar, banner e código de amigo) pode ser aberto por qualquer jogador que consiga ler o clã, porque o registo do clã guarda o identificador de jogador do líder; o código de amigo só serve para enviar um pedido de amizade, e podes bloquear qualquer pessoa. Guardamos também o próprio clã (o nome, a descrição escrita pelo líder, quantos membros tem, quando foi criado e que conta de jogador o lidera) e a semana em que recolheste pela última vez um prémio do raide. O nome, a descrição e o tamanho de um clã podem ser vistos e pesquisados por todos os jogadores que entraram nos Amigos; a lista dos membros não. Sair do clã apaga o teu lugar; quando sai o último membro, o clã é apagado. Um membro pode denunciar o nome ou a descrição do clã; a denúncia fica guardada como se descreve acima, contra o líder do clã, com o nome do clã.
- **O teu herói, mostrado aos teus amigos (ligado por defeito; podes escondê-lo).** Para os amigos poderem abrir o teu perfil, o jogo guarda no teu perfil um **retrato** pequeno do teu herói (o cabelo, a barba e o peitoral que vestes; o capacete só se escolheres mostrá-lo) e uma **ficha do herói** que só os teus amigos podem ler: o **aspeto** completo do teu herói (cabelo, barba e as peças cosméticas que vestes), o **equipamento que realmente vestes** (em cada ranhura: a peça, a raridade e o material), os teus cinco **atributos** (números) e o teu **clã** (só o identificador, para mostrar o nome). É só informação e não dá nada no jogo. Em **Definições → Amigos** podes desligá-lo («Mostrar o meu herói aos amigos»: a ficha e o retrato são apagados do servidor e o teu perfil volta a ter um avatar de cor simples) e escolher se o retrato mostra o capacete.
- **Avisos no telemóvel (uma escolha à parte, desligada por defeito).** Só se ligares **«Avisos no telemóvel»** nos Amigos e permitires as notificações no Android, o jogo pede ao **Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google)** um **código do aparelho** (um código que identifica o teu telemóvel para os avisos, não a ti) e guarda-o no **Cloud Firestore**, para uma função do servidor te poder avisar quando recebes um pedido de amizade ou um presente, mesmo com o jogo fechado. A mensagem que passa pela Google leva **só o tipo** (pedido ou presente): nada de nomes, textos ou códigos de amigo. O texto que vês é escrito pelo jogo no teu telemóvel, na tua língua, e por defeito não aparece nenhum aviso entre as 22h e as 8h. Para evitar abusos, o servidor guarda também, por jogador, uns contadores pequenos (o dia, quantos avisos foram enviados nesse dia e quando foi enviado o último de cada tipo). Desligar os avisos nos Amigos apaga o código no teu telemóvel e no nosso servidor; **«Apagar os meus dados sociais…»** ou apagar a conta apaga também os contadores.
- **Não há conversas.** Os jogadores não podem escrever mensagens uns aos outros; os únicos textos livres são a alcunha e o nome e a descrição de um clã.
- **Durante quanto tempo.** Até apagares. **Definições → Amigos → «Apagar os meus dados sociais…»** apaga o teu perfil, o código de amigo, as amizades, os pedidos, os presentes por apanhar, os bloqueios, os teus passos do ranking, a tua ficha e o teu retrato do herói, o código do aparelho dos avisos e os contadores, o teu lugar no clã, o registo dos teus prémios do raide e a própria conta de jogador. As denúncias são a exceção descrita acima.
- **Quem trata:** a Google, como prestador de serviços do programador. Ver https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
- **Porquê (base legal):** o teu consentimento, que dás ao entrar na função e criar um perfil, e que retiras ao apagar os teus dados.

### 6. Permissões
Atividade física (sensor de passos), passos do Health Connect (só leitura), notificações (os alertas do jogo e, só se os ligares, os avisos de pedidos de amizade e presentes), vibração e **Internet (só para os relatórios anónimos de falhas, a cópia opcional na nuvem, a função opcional Amigos, as compras através da Google Play e descarregar as novidades do jogo)**. A app não tem permissão de localização, contactos, câmara, microfone nem armazenamento.

### 7. Crianças
A app não se destina a crianças com menos de 13 anos. A função opcional Amigos não tem conversas; os únicos textos que os outros jogadores vêem são a alcunha e o nome e a descrição de um clã, que podem ser denunciados.
